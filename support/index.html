Sober Milestones Support

One day at a time. For help, email sobermilestones.support@gmail.com.

Getting started

Sign in with a phone verification code, set your sobriety date and open Friends to add manual friends or opt into contact discovery. Internet access is needed for sign-in, profile changes and connected-friend features.

Contacts and privacy

Contact discovery is optional. Change permissions in iPhone Settings. In Profile & Privacy, choose discoverability and what accepted friends can see. Removing a connection or blocking a person stops supported new access; copies already saved by others cannot be recalled.

Lock Screen widget

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, choose Customize and Add Widgets, then select Sober Milestones. Open the app after changing your date. iOS controls widget refresh timing.

Local reminders

Optional milestone reminders require notification permission. Check iPhone Settings if reminders are unavailable. Delivery timing is controlled by iOS.

Protect your data

Do not uninstall as a troubleshooting step: local dates, manual friends and photos may be lost. Signing out is not account deletion.

Report a profile or photo

Report abusive, threatening, sexually explicit or otherwise objectionable profile content to sobermilestones.support@gmail.com. Include the profile identifier and what needs review. Do not send your sobriety history, verification codes or other people’s private data. You can also block a person to remove the connection and stop supported requests.

Account deletion

The account-deletion service is not yet active during launch preparation. Please contact support about deletion requests; do not assume deletion completed unless confirmed.

Open Profile & Privacy → Delete Account and review the confirmation. The app reports completion only after the service confirms deletion. If deletion is unconfirmed, local records are kept; contact support before assuming that the server account still exists.

Contact support safely

Include your iPhone model, iOS/app version and steps to reproduce. Never send passwords, verification codes, full contact lists or private friend data.
