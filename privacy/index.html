Sober Milestones Privacy Policy

Publisher: Elliot Organ. Prepared November 15, 2026.

Information and purposes

Your phone number supports sign-in. Account information includes an identifier, display name and, if you choose, a profile photo and sobriety date. Sobriety information is health-related information. These are used to provide the app’s features, not advertising or cross-app tracking.

Optional contact discovery sends normalized phone numbers from the contacts you permit to the service for mutual matching. It does not send only device-generated hashes. The service stores matching information and account relationships. Manually added friends and their photos stay on your iPhone.

Your choices

You control contact discoverability and sharing of your date, milestones and photo with accepted friends. You can remove/block connections and change Contacts permission in iPhone Settings. Information already viewed or saved by another person cannot be recalled.

Storage and providers

Supabase hosts account/profile and connection features in the United States. Twilio Verify processes phone verification messages. The app and widget share local records through an App Group. Local records may be lost on uninstall. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept to provide the service until account deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements. Initiate deletion in Profile & Privacy → Delete Account. For security, sign out and sign in again immediately before starting deletion. An unconfirmed request does not establish whether deletion completed; contact support before retrying or signing in again. Confirmed deletion removes the account-linked active records and, on the initiating iPhone, that account’s local records and widget snapshot. Other accounts and legacy records stay untouched. Other devices may retain local copies until cleared. Backup and security-log copies can persist according to provider retention schedules; deletion does not promise immediate erasure of backups.

Backup and security-log copies may remain under the providers’ retention schedules after active account deletion. Phone verification and support correspondence are handled separately by their providers. Deleting the app or your app account does not promise immediate erasure of provider backups, security logs, verification records or messages you sent to support. Contact us about applicable retention or a privacy request.

Notifications

Milestone reminders are optional and scheduled on your iPhone. Names and milestone information may appear in notifications depending on your iPhone settings. Enhanced remote reminders are not enabled in this release.

Website

This static site has no advertising, analytics scripts or contact form. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub’s privacy practices. Do not send account credentials through this website.

Content reports and support

If you choose to report a profile, your email can include the profile identifier and your description of the concern. Your email provider also sends your return email address. The app does not automatically send reports or attach sobriety records or photos. Do not include verification codes or unnecessary personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions or unconfirmed deletion, contact sobermilestones.support@gmail.com. Do not email passwords or verification codes.
